### Bidikan

- **A (orang pertama):** penonton adalah orang Balik dari Part 1; hanya tangan dan lengannya yang terlihat, masuk dari bawah bingkai setinggi dada. Usia tangan mengikuti babak.
- **B (sorotan tokoh):** Orang Balik Muda, Orang Balik Tua, atau Tetangga Transmigran terlihat utuh.

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Orang Balik Muda** (posisi "kamu", Babak 1 sampai 3)

```
Orang Balik Muda is a Balik man of about forty, a farmer and forest gatherer in Sepaku, East Kalimantan, in the 1970s; he is the boy who fled there in 1942, now grown. Lean, wiry build, warm brown weathered skin, an angular face, large dark almond-shaped eyes under straight thick brows, a small flat nose, full lips, a thin black moustache, and a small old scar on the left knee. Short black hair, neatly cropped. He wears a short-sleeved collared cotton shirt in faded khaki with two chest pockets, open over a white singlet, and a plain dark green cotton sarong folded at the waist over knee-length black shorts; worn rubber sandals. A plain silver ring on his right ring finger and a working machete (parang) in a wooden sheath at his hip. Steady and slow, often stands with his arms folded.
```

**Orang Balik Tua** (posisi "kamu", Babak 5)

```
Orang Balik Tua is the same Balik man at eighty-five, in Sepaku, East Kalimantan, in 2019. Thin, slightly stooped frame, deeply wrinkled warm brown skin with age spots, an angular face with hollow cheeks, the same large dark almond-shaped eyes now slightly clouded, straight brows gone grey, a thin white moustache and white stubble. Short white hair under a faded black velvet peci cap. He wears a short-sleeved cream batik shirt with a small brown leaf-and-flower motif, a checked sarong in dark blue and maroon, and worn rubber sandals. A plain silver ring hangs loose on his right ring finger; he leans on a plain wooden walking stick and keeps a small notched wooden plank, darkened with age. Dignified and watchful, moves slowly.
```

**Tetangga Transmigran**

```
Tetangga Transmigran is a Javanese man of about thirty-five from East Java, a transmigrant farmer who arrives in Sepaku, East Kalimantan, in 1975. Stocky medium build, light brown skin, a broad friendly face, narrow cheerful eyes, a wide smile with slightly uneven teeth, and short side-parted black hair. He wears a short-sleeved white cotton shirt tucked into loose grey trousers rolled to the shin, with a woven bamboo conical hat (caping) slung on his back on a cord, and rubber sandals; he usually carries a hoe over one shoulder. Open and eager, gestures with his hands when he talks.
```
