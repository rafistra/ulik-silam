### Bidikan 9:16

- Utamakan bidikan sedang dan sedang-lebar, wajah di sekitar tengah gambar dengan ruang di atas kepala.
- Adegan bertekst layar (1 dan 7) punya ruang alami di atas: langit sore dan dinding kamar yang gelap.
- Bidikan A (orang pertama): tangan "kamu" masuk dari bawah bingkai setinggi dada, benda yang dipegang terlihat jelas di tengah. Bidikan B: sorotan tokoh dengan sudut kamera bervariasi (sejajar mata, dari bawah, dari balik bahu).

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Nyai** (posisi "kamu"; busana Babak 2–4)

```
Nyai, a Javanese woman of about 20 from a poor village near Semarang, living in the 1880s as the unmarried housekeeper and companion of a Dutch clerk. Slender and of medium height, with warm brown skin; oval face with soft cheekbones, dark almond-shaped eyes, straight thick black brows, a small nose with a low bridge, full lips held closed. Long black hair combed smoothly back into a low round bun at the nape, with no hair ornaments. She wears a long-sleeved, hip-length white cotton kebaya whose front edges and cuffs are trimmed with fine white European lace, closed with three small plain gold brooches, over an ankle-length hip-wrapped batik sarong in indigo blue and soga brown with a coastal pattern of small scattered flowers and storks; barefoot indoors. One thin plain gold bangle on her left wrist. She carries herself quietly upright, eyes often lowered, hands kept close to her body.
```

Varian Babak 1 (adegan 1–3): kebaya katun lurik bergaris cokelat-krem yang pudar, kain batik cokelat usang, tanpa alas kaki, tanpa perhiasan.

**Tuan**

```
Tuan, a Dutch trading-company clerk of about 36 in 1880s Semarang. Tall and lean with slightly stooped shoulders; pale skin reddened by the tropical sun; a long narrow face, light grey-blue eyes, pale brows, a straight thin nose and thin lips under a neatly trimmed sandy-brown moustache; short sandy-brown hair parted on the side. At home he wears a collarless white cotton shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbow and loose ankle-length trousers of thin batik cloth printed with red and blue flowers, with leather slippers; for official business, a white closed-collar cotton jacket buttoned to the throat, matching white trousers and black leather shoes. A silver watch chain; often a cigar in hand. He moves slowly and deliberately, chin slightly raised, rarely looking directly at the people who serve him.
```

**Anak**

```
Anak, a Javanese-Dutch boy of about two to three. Small and round-cheeked, with light olive skin clearly lighter than his mother's, soft light-brown curly hair, large dark brown eyes and a small nose. He wears a loose knee-length white cotton smock with short sleeves and goes barefoot; in some scenes he also wears a small white cloth European-style cap. No jewelry. Clings to adults and reaches out with chubby hands; curious and often sleepy.
```

Di adegan 7 anak masih bayi baru lahir dan dibungkus kain putih; tidak memakai lembar Anak.

### Figuran

Ayah, abdi tua, pembantu muda, tamu Belanda, anak-anak Indo di pasar, pedagang, dan pejabat tampil tanpa lembar karakter. Masing-masing diberi usia, perawakan, dan busana yang berbeda dari tokoh utama, tanpa renda putih, bros emas, atau batik bermotif bangau milik Nyai. Setiap prompt yang memuat figuran punya kalimat pembeda, dan kalimat "Avoid ..." memuat "background figures copying Nyai's face or outfit".

### Dramatisasi

- "Kamu", Tuan, dan anaknya fiktif, disusun dari pola yang tercatat; nama Belanda anak sengaja tidak disebut.
- Pikiran dan perasaan "kamu", alasan memohon pengakuan, dan adegan pasar adalah dramatisasi yang selaras dengan sumber.
- Usia Tuan ("dua kali usiamu") dan jeda "dua tahun kemudian" adalah rekaan.
- "Kamu" dewasa sejak adegan pertama; hubungan dengan Tuan tersirat.
