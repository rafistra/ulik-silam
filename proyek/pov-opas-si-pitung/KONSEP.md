### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Opas**

```
Opas, a fictional Betawi native police assistant (oppas) in Batavia, 1892 to 1893, about 25 years old. Lean wiry build, medium-brown sun-darkened skin, oval face with high cheekbones, dark watchful almond eyes under straight thick brows, broad nose, thin lips, clean-shaven, short black hair. Clothing from head to foot: a dark indigo cotton iket head cloth tied flat with a small knot at the back; a plain dark indigo collarless cotton jacket with long sleeves, narrow buttoned cuffs and small brass buttons down the front; a worn brown leather belt over the jacket with a small cartridge pouch; loose dark indigo knee-length trousers; barefoot. Carries an old single-shot rifle with a plain wooden stock. No jewelry. Holds himself stiffly, shoulders slightly hunched, eyes often glancing sideways.
```

**Schout Hinne**

```
Schout A.W.V. Hinne, Dutch colonial police chief of the Tanah Abang district in Batavia, early 1890s, in his late thirties (face invented, no portrait known). Tall and heavy-set, ruddy sunburned pale skin, square jaw, small pale grey eyes, thick drooping light-brown moustache, short receding light-brown hair. Clothing from head to foot: a white cotton cap with a short black visor; a white high-collared closed tunic with a single row of brass buttons; a black leather belt with a revolver holster on the right hip and a sabre on the left; white trousers; black leather ankle boots; a pocket-watch chain across the chest. Moves restlessly, leans forward when listening, jaw often clenched.
```

**Si Pitung**

```
Si Pitung (real name Salihoen), Betawi bandit from Rawa Belong, Batavia, early 1890s, in his late twenties (face invented, no portrait known; costume follows the popular Betawi depiction). Medium height, compact muscular build, warm brown skin, oval face, sharp dark eyes under thick brows, a thin black moustache, short black hair. Clothing from head to foot: a black kopiah (rimless felt cap); a loose deep red pangsi shirt with a round collarless neck and long wide sleeves over a plain white undershirt; a wide green cloth sash tied at the waist; loose deep red pangsi trousers ending at mid-calf; a checked sarong in muted red and brown draped around the neck, its folds hanging over the left shoulder; brown leather terompah sandals. A short golok in a plain wooden sheath tucked into the left side of the sash; a dark akar bahar bracelet on the right wrist. Stands loose and relaxed with his weight on one leg, often with a half smile.
```

Varian adegan 11–12: celana pendek hitam selutut, sabuk peluru kulit, tanpa sarung, memegang revolver.

### Dramatisasi

- "Kamu" adalah opas rekaan; kehadirannya di setiap adegan, perasaannya, dan apakah ia opas yang menembak di adegan 12 sengaja tidak dipastikan.
- Tanggal pasti kematian tidak disebut: Hindia Olanda menyebut Pitung mati pada hari Sabtu, dan Liputan6 menyebut laporan 14 Oktober 1893; 14 Oktober 1893 jatuh pada hari Sabtu, tetapi ini tafsir.
- Tempat makam berbeda antarsumber (Kampung Baru, Krekot, rancak menyebut Penjaringan, kini di Kebayoran Lama), jadi narasi tidak menyebut lokasinya.
- Wajah Pitung, Hinne, dan Dji-ih rekaan; tidak ditemukan potret masa itu.
- Posisi moral seimbang: perampokan bersenjata dan pembunuhan pelapor disebut, begitu pula penghormatan kampung dan cara polisi.

## Ide cadangan

- **Pembantu di rumah Haji Sapiudin, Marunda (fiktif).** Malam perampokan 30 Juli 1892 dari sisi korban; epilog rumah itu kini disebut "Rumah Si Pitung". Hook: "Malam itu, rumah majikanmu dirampok. Seratus tahun kemudian, rumah itu diberi nama perampoknya."
- **Juru tulis Hindia Olanda (fiktif).** Menyalin laporan polisi, melihat ejaan Bitoeng berubah menjadi Si Pitoeng, sampai menulis berita kematiannya. Hook: "Kamu yang pertama menulis namanya. Dan kamu salah mengejanya."
- **Pemuda Rawa Belong, tetangga Pitung (fiktif).** Mengenalnya sejak kecil sampai kampung berkabung; paling bergantung pada kisah tradisional.
- **Si Pitung sendiri.** Kuat secara dramatis, tetapi hidupnya sangat sedikit tercatat.
- **Alur hitung mundur menuju penyergapan Oktober 1893.** Beberapa hari terakhir sebelum kuburan Tanah Abang.
- **Akhir alternatif: senapan yang tak ditembakkan.** Saat penyergapan, kamu ragu menarik pelatuk.
