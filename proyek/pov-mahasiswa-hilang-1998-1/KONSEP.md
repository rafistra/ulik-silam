### Bidikan 9:16

- **Bidikan A (orang pertama):** penonton adalah Mahasiswa; tangan masuk dari bawah bingkai setinggi dada. Dipakai di 9 dari 14 adegan supaya penonton "berada di dalam" ketegangan.

- Orang tak dikenal di adegan 13 selalu siluet tanpa wajah dan tanpa seragam, logo, atau atribut lembaga apa pun.

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Mahasiswa** ("kamu"; tokoh rekaan)

```
Mahasiswa, a 21-year-old Javanese university student from a farming family in Klaten, studying in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, 1998. Slim, slightly underfed build; warm light-brown skin; oval face with soft cheekbones, thick black eyebrows, dark tired but alert eyes, a straight nose, full lips, a faint shadow of a thin moustache; thick black hair grown shaggy to the collar, parted loosely in the middle. Wears an unbuttoned faded blue-and-grey checked flannel shirt with the sleeves rolled to the forearm over a plain white crew-neck T-shirt, faded light-blue straight-cut jeans, and worn black rubber sandals. A cheap black digital wristwatch on the left wrist. Carries a black canvas backpack. No jewelry, no visible logos or text on clothing. Moves with nervous energy, often glancing over his shoulder, shoulders slightly hunched.
```

**Kawan Gondrong** (kawan aktivis; rekaan)

```
Kawan Gondrong, a 23-year-old male Javanese student activist, Yogyakarta and Jakarta, 1998. Tall and lanky; medium-brown skin; long narrow face, round thin wire-framed glasses, sparse chin goatee; long wavy black hair tied in a low ponytail. Wears an olive-green army-surplus field jacket over a plain black T-shirt with no print, dark blue jeans, and scuffed canvas sneakers. Often holds a clove cigarette or a stack of photocopied papers. Calm, sardonic, sits with one knee up.
```

**Kawan Perempuan** (kawan aktivis; rekaan)

```
Kawan Perempuan, a 22-year-old female Javanese student activist, Yogyakarta and Jakarta, 1998. Small and wiry; warm medium-brown skin; round face, sharp dark eyes, no makeup; straight black hair tied in a practical low ponytail with loose strands. Wears a plain off-white long-sleeved cotton shirt with sleeves pushed up, dark brown loose trousers, and simple brown sandals; carries a woven cloth tote bag full of leaflets. Determined and quick, often writing in a small notebook.
```
