**Penutup prompt:**

```
Style: detailed vector art
Aspect ratio: 9:16
```

**Panduan bidikan 9:16.** Utamakan bidikan sedang dan sedang-lebar, wajah di sekitar tengah, ruang di atas kepala. Adegan bertekst layar (1, 2, 4, 11, 13, 15, 17) memakai isi adegan yang lapang di atas: langit fajar, lambung kapal, langit mendung, langit penuh payung.

**Bidikan A** (orang pertama): adegan 1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 14. **Bidikan B** (sorotan tokoh): adegan lain. Adegan 16 memakai foto asli.

**Deskripsi tokoh baku**

- **Romusha Surabaya** ("kamu"): Romusha Surabaya: a Javanese woman of about 20 from Surabaya, a commoner forced laborer on Noemfoor Island in 1944. Slight, undernourished build with visible collarbones; warm medium-brown skin; oval face with high cheekbones, dark almond-shaped eyes, thick straight brows, a broad soft nose and full lips cracked from thirst. Long black hair twisted into a loose low bun at the nape, with strands falling free. She wears a plain hip-length off-white cotton kebaya blouse, now torn and stained grey-brown, its sleeves ripped ragged just below the elbow, held closed with two plain safety pins; around her hips, a faded brown cotton kain wrap with a small stamped motif of scattered dots and tiny flowers almost washed out, its hem frayed and torn to mid-calf; a thin strip of burlap sacking tied around her waist as a belt. Barefoot, her feet cut by coral. No jewelry. She moves with guarded, watchful alertness, shoulders slightly hunched, always half-turned toward the younger brother she protects.
- **Adik:** Adik: a Javanese boy of 13 from Surabaya, the younger brother of Romusha Surabaya, a forced laborer on Noemfoor Island in 1944. Small, very thin frame with ribs and elbows showing; warm medium-brown skin a shade lighter than his sister's; a round face with large dark eyes, short straight brows, a small nose and chapped lips. Short black hair roughly cropped close to the scalp, uneven. Bare-chested, with a faded white cotton shirt far too big for him knotted around his waist; knee-length dark grey cotton shorts, torn at one leg and held up with a twisted cloth cord. Barefoot, with coral scratches on his shins. No jewelry, no headwear. He stays close to his sister, often clutching the edge of her sleeve, his curious eyes darting around even when he is afraid.

Di adegan 2 dan 3 (Surabaya dan kapal), keduanya masih berpakaian bersih dan utuh; prompt adegan menyebut perubahan itu.

**Tangan baku "kamu"**

- **Tangan baku · Surabaya (adegan 2–3):** Her hands are slim with warm medium-brown skin, short clean nails and lightly calloused palms; the long sleeves of a plain, clean off-white cotton kebaya reach her wrists; no rings, no bracelets.
- **Tangan baku · Numfor (adegan 1, 4–17):** Her hands are slim and bony with warm medium-brown skin, cracked dry knuckles scratched by coral and short broken nails with dirt beneath them; the sleeves of a torn, grey-brown-stained off-white cotton kebaya end in ragged edges just below the elbow, showing thin forearms; no rings, no bracelets.

**Figuran**

- **Pekerja Jawa:** laki-laki, perempuan, anak, dan remaja dari beragam usia dan perawakan; pakaian lusuh dengan warna lain dari tokoh utama (hijau pudar, abu-abu, cokelat tua), tanpa sabuk goni dan tanpa motif kain "kamu".
- **Buruh Formosa:** laki-laki Asia Timur beragam usia, sangat kurus, kemeja kerja abu-abu dan celana pendek usang, sebagian bertopi jerami (tafsir).
- **Tentara Jepang:** seragam tropis katun hijau pudar, topi kain dengan penutup leher atau helm baja berjaring, pembalut betis, sepatu bot rendah, senapan. Perwira membawa pedang shin guntō.
- **Tentara Sekutu (adegan 14–15):** penerjun di bawah payung bundar; di kamp, kemeja khaki dan topi lapangan (tafsir).

Setiap prompt dengan figuran memuat kalimat pembeda dan "background figures copying Romusha Surabaya's or Adik's face or outfit, identical faces" di kalimat Avoid. Gore (luka terbuka, cipratan darah) masuk Avoid di semua prompt, sesuai batas kekerasan.

**Templat prompt adegan:** kalimat "Same character as the <Nama tokoh> reference image." atau "The viewer is Romusha Surabaya ..."; bidikan, tempat, waktu; untuk bidikan A "First-person point of view." + tangan baku + kerja setiap tangan; gerak, ekspresi, arah pandang setiap tokoh; figuran dan kalimat pembeda; "Detailed vector art illustration, clean crisp outlines, flat colors with subtle shading"; palet babak; "historically accurate ..."; kalimat Avoid; baris kosong; penutup prompt.

## Prompt ilustrasi untuk Gemini

Cara pakai: buat dua lembar karakter dulu dan simpan dengan namanya. Unggah lembar tokoh yang tampil di setiap adegan, termasuk lembar Romusha Surabaya di setiap bidikan A (1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 14). Kerjakan dalam satu percakapan Gemini, minta ubah ke 9:16 bila hasilnya tidak vertikal. Bila Gemini menolak adegan 7, 8, atau 11, minta bidikan yang sama dari jarak lebih jauh atau dari belakang kerumunan, tanpa mengubah narasi.
