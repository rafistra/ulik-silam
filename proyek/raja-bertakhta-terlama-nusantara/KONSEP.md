### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Pakubuwono XII** (lembar karakter; wajah tampil di adegan 6 dan 7): Pakubuwono XII, Susuhunan of Surakarta, about 20 years old in 1945, Javanese. Slim, upright build, light brown skin, smooth oval face, calm dark almond eyes, straight brows, short neat black hair under his headgear, clean-shaven, youthful but reserved look. Coronation dress: a tall black velvet kuluk: a brimless cap shaped like a truncated cone narrowing to a flat top, with gold braided trim along the bottom edge, a horizontal gold braid band about two-thirds up, vertical gold braid strips dividing the upper part into panels, and a small beaded gold knob on top; a dark close-fitting high-collared court jacket with dense gold embroidery along the edges and cuffs; a very large wrapped ceremonial waist-cloth with bold diagonal blade-shaped stripes (parang barong) around a plain diamond-shaped centre panel, its long pointed tail trailing behind; patterned silk trousers (cindhe) visible below; slippers. A keris worn at the back of the waist, small ornamental gold ear pieces. Sits and stands very still and composed, hands resting on his knees, speaking little.

Tokoh lain yang wajahnya tampil sekali (Syafiuddin II di adegan 3, Sulaiman remaja di adegan 9 dan dewasa di adegan 10, Panembahan Ratu di adegan 12, Wastu Kancana kecil di adegan 15 dan dewasa di adegan 16) dideskripsikan lengkap di prompt adegannya. Wajah semua raja adalah rekaan, karena tidak ada potret yang dibuka.

**Prasasti Kawali I** (lembar rujukan; tampil di adegan 16 dan 17): Prasasti Kawali I, an Old Sundanese stone inscription from Kawali, West Java, 14th century. A broad, flat, roughly trapezoidal natural andesite slab, slightly taller than wide, reaching about the hip of an adult, only a hand-span thick; dark grey with green-grey lichen and moss patina; undressed, irregular natural edges; about ten uneven horizontal lines of shallow incised script on the broad face and a few short lines along the thick side edge, shown as faint illegible marks; set upright in the earth; no frame, no border, no polish.

### Figuran

Figuran selalu beragam usia dan perawakan, berbusana lebih sederhana dari raja, tanpa warna khas raja (kuning untuk Sambas dan Serdang, kuluk dan parang barong untuk Surakarta). Setiap prompt dengan figuran memuat kalimat pembeda dan "background figures copying ..." di kalimat Avoid.

### Templat prompt

```
<Rujukan lembar karakter atau lembar rujukan, bila ada>
<Bidikan dan sudut>, <tempat>, <masa>, <waktu dan cuaca>.
<Aksi, ekspresi, arah pandang tokoh>. <Figuran dan kalimat pembeda>.
Foreground: ...; middle ground: ...; background: .... <Cahaya dan unsur bergerak>.
Detailed vector art illustration, clean crisp outlines, flat colors with subtle shading, <palet babak>, historically accurate <era dan tempat>.
Avoid <anakronisme>, characters looking at the camera, extra or malformed hands and fingers, text, letters, watermarks.

Style: detailed vector art
Aspect ratio: 9:16
```

Cara pakai: buat lembar karakter Pakubuwono XII (unggah foto referensi kuluk bersama promptnya) dan lembar rujukan Prasasti Kawali I lebih dulu, unggah lembar itu bersama prompt adegan yang merujuknya, kerjakan satu entri per percakapan Gemini, dan minta ubah ke 9:16 bila hasilnya tidak vertikal.
