# Konsep Video POV: Orang Iban di Ekspedisi Putussibau, 1894

## Ringkasan konsep

Seorang pemuda Iban dari Batang Lupar ikut ekspedisi Belanda 1894 yang berangkat dari Putussibau untuk menyeberang ke Mahakam. Ia satu dari delapan orang Iban di tengah delapan puluh lima pendayung Kayan, kaum yang sudah lama bermusuhan dengan kaumnya. Rombongan sempat melewati batas air ke lembah Mahakam, lalu berbalik karena sebuah kabar.

### Bidikan

- **A (orang pertama):** penonton adalah Pemuda Iban; hanya tangan dan lengannya yang terlihat, masuk dari bawah bingkai setinggi dada, biasanya memegang tali keranjang pikul, dayung, atau nasi.
- **B (sorotan tokoh):** Pemuda Iban, Pemuda Kayan, Kontrolir, dan Dokter Muda terlihat utuh.
- Utamakan bidikan sedang atau sedang-lebar dengan wajah di sekitar tengah gambar. Untuk adegan bertekst layar (1, 2, 9, 13, 15), isi adegan memberi ruang di bagian atas: langit, kabut, tajuk hutan.

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Pemuda Iban** (posisi "kamu")

```
Pemuda Iban is an Iban man of about twenty from the Batang Lupar river region of western Borneo, in 1894, a paddler and porter on a Dutch expedition. Lean, wiry build, warm medium-brown skin, an oval face with high cheekbones, lively dark eyes, thick straight brows, a broad nose and full lips. His thick black hair is cut in a straight square fringe across the forehead and falls long and loose down his back past the shoulders; the sides are not shaved. A row of small brass rings runs along the rim of each ear. On each shoulder, just below the collarbone, sits a dark blue round tattoo shaped like an eggplant blossom with a double spiral at its centre. He is bare-chested and wears a long waistcloth of faded red cotton wound around the waist and passed between the legs, its front flap hanging to the knees; barefoot. A plain white shell bracelet circles his right wrist, and a working machete in a plain wooden sheath hangs at his left hip from a rattan belt. Quick and watchful, always half-turned toward the others.
```

**Pemuda Kayan**

```
Pemuda Kayan is a Kayan man of about twenty from the Mendalam River in the upper Kapuas of western Borneo, in 1894, a paddler and porter on the same expedition. Sturdy, broad-shouldered build, light-brown skin, a round face, narrow serious eyes, straight brows, a flat nose and a firm mouth. His black hair is cut in a straight fringe across the forehead, the sides and crown are shaved, and the hair at the back hangs loose to the nape; a narrow band of plaited rattan circles his brow. His earlobes are stretched into long loops holding plain brass rings, and a small round wooden disc sits in the upper part of each ear. A dark blue curling dog-motif tattoo marks the front of each thigh below the waistcloth. He is bare-chested and wears a long waistcloth of plain dark blue cotton; barefoot. A plain dark hardwood bangle circles each wrist, and a sword in a plain wooden sheath hangs at his left hip from a rattan belt. Reserved and serious, slow to smile.
```

**Kontrolir**

```
Kontrolir is a Dutch colonial civil officer of about thirty-five, head of the upper Kapuas district in 1894. Medium height, slightly heavy build, pale skin reddened by the sun, a square face, light grey eyes, a full brown moustache and short brown hair. He wears a white cotton jacket with a high standing collar and a single row of brass buttons, white trousers, brown leather boots and a white cloth-covered sun helmet; a leather map case hangs from a strap across his chest. Stiff and upright, used to giving orders.
```

**Dokter Muda**

```
Dokter Muda is a Dutch army medical officer of about thirty, the expedition's doctor in 1894. Slim build, pale skin tanned by travel, a narrow face, attentive light eyes, a neat dark moustache and short dark hair. He wears a white cotton jacket with a high standing collar, sleeves pushed up to the forearm, white trousers, brown leather boots and a white sun helmet; a canvas satchel hangs at his hip and he carries a small notebook and pencil. Curious and restless, always looking closely at people and things.
```

### Tangan baku "kamu" (bidikan A)

```
Young man's lean hands with warm medium-brown skin, calloused palms from paddling, short nails, bare forearms with no tattoos, a plain white shell bracelet on the right wrist.
```

Satu varian untuk seluruh video. Lengan bawah sengaja tanpa tato: tato tangan (tegulun) pada orang Iban adalah tanda pengayau.

### Figuran

- **Pendayung dan pemikul Kayan lain:** usia dan perawakan beragam; rambut poni dengan sisi dicukur; cawat katun biru tua, putih kusam, atau kulit kayu; sebagian memakai topi lebar dari daun palem berbentuk payung jamur; tanpa gelang kayu gelap seperti Pemuda Kayan.
- **Tujuh orang Iban lain:** rambut panjang terurai berponi, cincin kecil di pinggir telinga, cawat merah tua atau biru; tanpa gelang kerang seperti "kamu"; berdiri atau duduk berkelompok, sedikit terpisah.
- **Perahu:** perahu lesung dari satu batang kayu, dinding ditinggikan dengan papan yang diikat; di hulu diberi lantai bambu belah; perahu orang Belanda beratap daun palem melengkung di bagian tengah.
- **Prajurit (adegan 2):** prajurit pribumi KNIL beragam usia dalam jas dan celana katun biru tua, topi kepi hitam, bertelanjang kaki, membawa senapan laras panjang sekali tembak.
- **Kuli Melayu:** sarung, ikat kepala, baju lengan panjang sederhana.
- **Ahli batuan (adegan 2 dan 8):** lelaki Belanda sekitar 34 tahun, berjanggut cokelat penuh, jas katun terang, topi lebar, palu geologi di tangan.
- **Utusan (adegan 10):** lelaki Kayan paruh baya, berkeringat dan terengah-engah, cawat putih kusam, tanpa ikat kepala.
- **Warga Putussibau (adegan 13, 15):** orang Melayu bersarung dan berpeci, pedagang Tionghoa berkemeja putih longgar, orang Taman dan Kayan di tepian.
- Setiap prompt dengan figuran memuat kalimat pembeda dan larangan menyalin wajah atau pakaian tokoh utama.
