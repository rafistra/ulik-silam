```
Radin, a Javanese santri (Islamic boarding-school student) in his twenties, from a poor farming family on the north coast of Central Java, mid-19th century. Slim, wiry build of a young man used to walking long distances; warm medium-brown skin; long oval face with prominent high cheekbones and a slightly tapering jaw, straight dark eyebrows, narrow dark attentive eyes, a broad nose, thin lips, noticeably large protruding ears, clean-shaven with no moustache or beard; short black hair hidden beneath the headcloth.
