## Ringkasan konsep

Seorang perempuan Homo erectus hidup bersama kawanan kecilnya di tepi Bengawan Solo, lebih dari seratus ribu tahun lalu, saat padang terbuka perlahan berganti hutan hujan. Kemarau panjang lalu banjir besar mengubur mereka di tikungan sungai. Epilog menyusul tengkorak itu ke tahun 1931 dan ke penanggalan modern yang menjadikan Ngandong jejak terakhir Homo erectus yang diketahui.

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Perempuan Soloensis** (posisi "kamu")

```
Perempuan Soloensis is an adult female Homo erectus of about twenty-five, from a small band living on the banks of the Solo River in Java more than 100,000 years ago. Short, lean and strong, about 158 cm tall, with dark brown skin and very little body hair. A long, low head with a receding forehead, heavy brow ridges arching separately over each deep-set dark eye, broad prominent cheekbones, a wide flat nose, a forward-projecting mouth and no chin; short, dark, tightly curled hair. She wears a simple untailored wrap of soft brown animal hide tied over one shoulder and belted at the waist with a twisted vine, reaching mid-thigh; barefoot. She carries a small sharp flake of translucent honey-brown chalcedony. Alert and protective, always scanning the grass and the water.
```

**Anak Soloensis**

```
Anak Soloensis is a Homo erectus child of about six from the same band. Small and thin, dark brown skin, a rounded low forehead with only faint brow ridges, large curious dark eyes, a wide flat nose and a small jaw without a chin; short, dark, tightly curled hair. A small wrap of soft brown hide around the hips; barefoot. A tiny chipped stone is often clutched in one fist. Curious and clinging, quick to copy the adults.
```

### Tangan baku "kamu" (bidikan A)

### Figuran

- **Anggota kawanan lain (adegan 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11):** empat sampai enam orang dewasa beragam usia dan perawakan, salah satunya tetua berambut keriting beruban dan berbahu bungkuk; penutup tubuh kulit hewan dengan warna berbeda (abu-cokelat, cokelat kemerahan), tanpa ikat sulur seperti "kamu"; membawa serpih batu atau batu bulat pemukul.

- **Peneliti (adegan 15):** hanya tangan bersarung dan jas laboratorium; wajah tidak ditampilkan.
- Setiap prompt dengan figuran memuat kalimat pembeda dan larangan menyalin wajah atau pakaian tokoh utama.
