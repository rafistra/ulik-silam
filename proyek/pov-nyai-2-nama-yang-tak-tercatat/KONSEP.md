### Bidikan 9:16

- Utamakan bidikan sedang dan sedang-lebar dengan wajah di sekitar tengah gambar.
- Adegan bertekst layar (1 dan 10) punya ruang alami di atas: atap beranda dan langit terbuka.
- Kontak mata dengan penonton hanya di adegan 3 (Anak ragu), 7 (Anak menjerit), dan 11 (Anak Besar tak mengenali); adegan lain memuat "characters looking at the camera" di kalimat Avoid.

### Deskripsi tokoh baku

Nyai, Tuan, dan Anak: sama persis dengan Part 1 (lihat lembar karakter di bawah). Di Babak 4 Nyai lebih kurus, berkebaya katun abu pudar yang rendanya terlepas, kain cokelat usang, tanpa perhiasan.

**Anak Besar**

```
Anak Besar, the same Javanese-Dutch boy at about eight years old, now raised in a Dutch household. Slim and long-limbed for his age, with light olive skin clearly lighter than his mother's, light-brown curly hair cut short and neatly combed with a side parting, large dark brown eyes, a small nose and a guarded, serious expression. He wears a white cotton shirt with a round collar, white knee-length short trousers, white knee socks and black leather shoes. No jewelry. He moves energetically while playing, but stiffens and becomes watchful around strangers.
```

### Tangan dan lengan "kamu" (bidikan A)

**Tangan baku · Babak 1–3** (adegan 3 dan 7; sama dengan panel tangan di lembar Nyai)

```
Slim hands with warm brown skin, smoother now, short clean nails; forearms covered by long white cotton kebaya sleeves edged at the cuff with fine white European lace; one thin plain gold bangle on the left wrist.
```

**Tangan baku · Babak 4** (adegan 11)

```
Thinner hands with warm brown skin, dry knuckles and short uneven nails, no jewelry; forearms covered by the frayed sleeves of a faded greyish cotton kebaya with the lace torn away at the cuffs.
```

### Figuran

Kerabat Tuan (pria Eropa gemuk berjenggot pirang keabuan, jas panjang hitam), perempuan Eropa berbaju hitam berkerudung pendek, para pelayat, dan pembantu laki-laki Jawa tampil tanpa lembar karakter. Masing-masing diberi kalimat pembeda dari Nyai dan Tuan, dan kalimat "Avoid ..." memuat "background figures copying Nyai's face or outfit". Adegan kekerasan (7 dan 8) memuat "blood, wounds" di kalimat Avoid.
