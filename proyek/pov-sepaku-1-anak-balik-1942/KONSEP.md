### Deskripsi tokoh baku

**Anak Balik** (posisi "kamu")

```
Anak Balik is a young boy of the Balik people, a small Muslim indigenous community of the East Kalimantan coast, in January 1942. Slim, wiry child's build with warm brown sun-tanned skin, a round face, large dark almond-shaped eyes under straight thick brows, a small flat nose, full lips, and a small healed scar on the left knee. Short black hair, cropped close and slightly uneven. He wears a faded collarless loose cotton shirt in dull off-white with short sleeves and knee-length plain dark brown cotton shorts tied with a cord; barefoot. A folded checked cotton sarong in faded red and dark brown is tied loosely around his waist like a sash. He often holds a small hand-cut wooden plank the length of his forearm, pale ironwood colour, with one notch carved at the top. Alert, watchful posture; restless hands that grip whatever they hold.
```

**Ayah**

```
Ayah is a Balik man of about thirty-five, a plank maker on the East Kalimantan coast in January 1942. Lean, muscular build with broad calloused hands, warm deep brown skin, a long face with high cheekbones, deep-set dark eyes, thick brows, a short sparse black moustache and weathered forehead lines. Short black hair under a red cotton head wrap (laung) tied flat around the head with the knot at the back. He wears a loose collarless long-sleeved cotton shirt in faded indigo with the sleeves rolled to the elbows, and a plain dark brown cotton sarong hitched to the knee over short black trousers; barefoot. A small working machete (parang) in a plain wooden sheath hangs at his left hip. Quiet and stiffly upright, jaw often set, few words.
```

**Ibu**

```
Ibu is a Balik woman of about thirty, wife of a plank maker on the East Kalimantan coast in January 1942. Slight build, warm brown skin, an oval face, soft dark eyes, thin arched brows and a small mouth. Her hair is in a low bun under a loosely draped plain cream cotton head cloth. She wears a long loose long-sleeved blouse reaching her hips, in the baju kurung style, in faded dusty green cotton without embroidery, over an ankle-length woven cotton sarong with a simple dark red and black checked pattern tied with a fold at the side; barefoot. A small plain brass ring on her right hand. Calm and protective, hands always busy.
```
