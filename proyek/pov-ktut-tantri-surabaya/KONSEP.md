**Penutup prompt:**

```
Style: detailed vector art
Aspect ratio: 9:16
```

**Panduan bidikan 9:16.** Utamakan bidikan sedang dan sedang-lebar dengan wajah di sekitar tengah gambar dan ruang di atas kepala. Adegan bertekst layar (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 13) memakai isi adegan yang lapang di atas: dinding gelap studio, langit Bali, langit pelabuhan, asap, langit penuh selebaran, langit fajar.

**Bidikan A** (orang pertama): adegan 1, 5, 7, 12, 14. **Bidikan B** (sorotan tokoh): adegan lain. Kontak mata yang disengaja hanya di adegan 1, 12, dan 14 (Teknisi Radio menatap "kamu").

**Deskripsi tokoh baku**

- **K'tut Tantri** ("kamu"): K'tut Tantri: a Scottish-born white woman of about 47, born Muriel Stuart Walker, an English-language broadcaster for the Indonesian Republican fighters in Surabaya in 1945. Small and slight, noticeably short; fair skin tanned and lightly freckled by years in the tropics; a narrow face with a pointed chin, lively grey-green eyes, thin arched brows, a straight nose and thin lips, fine lines at the corners of the eyes. Hair dyed black, shoulder length, pinned back loosely at the nape with a few strands escaping, a faint coppery-red tint showing at the roots. She wears a plain short-sleeved cotton blouse in faded khaki with a small pointed collar and two buttoned breast pockets, tucked into a mid-calf A-line skirt of dark olive cotton drill, a narrow brown leather belt, and flat brown leather sandals. A slim brown leather wristwatch on her left wrist; no other jewelry, no make-up. Quick, restless hands; she leans forward when she speaks, chin raised, alert and defiant.
- **Teknisi Radio** (rekaan): Teknisi Radio: a Javanese young man of about 20 from Surabaya, the radio technician of the Republican fighters' secret broadcasting station in 1945. Lean, wiry build; warm medium-brown skin; an angular face with high cheekbones, dark deep-set eyes, thick straight brows, a broad nose and a faint shadow of a mustache. Short black hair, side-parted and combed back. He wears a white short-sleeved cotton shirt with the sleeves rolled up tight above the elbow, open at the collar, a small red-and-white cloth badge pinned above the left breast pocket, knee-length khaki cotton shorts with a plain canvas belt, and black canvas shoes without socks. A small screwdriver clipped in his breast pocket and a pair of 1940s bakelite headphones hanging around his neck. He moves fast and economically, always adjusting a dial or a wire, glancing often toward the door.

Di adegan 2 (kilas Bali, 1930-an), Tantri tampil sekitar sepuluh tahun lebih muda, berbaju katun putih lengan panjang dan berkain nila pudar, tanpa alas kaki; prompt adegan menyebut perubahan itu.

**Tangan baku "kamu" (semua bidikan A, adegan 1, 5, 7, 12, 14):** Her hands are small and slender with fair, sun-tanned and lightly freckled skin, prominent knuckles and short unpainted nails, a faint ink stain on the right middle finger; the short sleeves of a faded khaki cotton blouse end above the elbow, showing thin forearms; a slim brown leather wristwatch on the left wrist; no rings.

**Figuran**

- **Pemuda pejuang:** pemuda Jawa dan Madura beragam usia (16 sampai 30), tinggi, dan perawakan; kemeja katun polos dan celana pendek atau panjang, sebagian bertelanjang kaki, sebagian berikat kepala merah putih, beberapa bertopi lapangan Jepang rampasan atau helm baja Belanda bercat merah putih; membawa senapan Arisaka atau bambu runcing. Tidak memakai lencana, headphone, atau kemeja putih berlengan tergulung seperti Teknisi Radio.
- **Serdadu India Brigade 49:** beragam usia dan perawakan, kemeja dan celana hijau hutan (jungle green), helm baja model Inggris, sabuk dan tali perlengkapan, senapan Lee-Enfield; ekspresinya manusiawi (penasaran, letih), bukan penjahat tanpa wajah.
- **Warga Surabaya:** perempuan berkebaya dan berkain jarik, laki-laki berkemeja dan berpeci, anak-anak; tanpa pakaian khaki seperti Tantri.
- **Perempuan Bali 1930-an (adegan 2):** kain katun dan selendang, membawa sesajen buah dan bunga di kepala.

Setiap prompt dengan figuran memuat kalimat pembeda dan "background figures copying K'tut Tantri's or Teknisi Radio's face or outfit, identical faces" di kalimat Avoid. Gore dan jenazah masuk Avoid di adegan pertempuran, sesuai kolom Hindari.

**Templat prompt adegan:** kalimat "Same character as the <Nama tokoh> reference image." atau "The viewer is K'tut Tantri ..."; bidikan, tempat, waktu; untuk bidikan A "First-person point of view." + tangan baku + kerja setiap tangan; gerak, ekspresi, dan arah pandang setiap tokoh; figuran dan kalimat pembeda; "Detailed vector art illustration, clean crisp outlines, flat colors with subtle shading"; palet babak; "historically accurate ..."; kalimat Avoid; baris kosong; penutup prompt.

**Referensi visual.** Foto-foto Tantri ada di memoarnya, *Revolt in Paradise* (1960), tetapi belum ada potret berlisensi bebas yang bisa dibuka. Bila Rafi punya salinan foto dari buku itu, unggah ke Gemini bersama prompt lembar karakter dengan kalimat tambahan: "Use the uploaded photo only as a reference for her face shape and hairstyle, not for the pose or art style." Foto IWM SE 5866 dan SE 5742 (Surabaya, November–Desember 1945, keterangannya dibaca di Last Stand on Zombie Island) bisa dicari di Wikimedia Commons dan diunggah sebagai referensi kendaraan dan suasana jalan di adegan 13.
