Pak Harun, a Javanese man in his late forties, a regular visitor of Kramat Tunggak in December 1999. Stocky with a round belly, brown skin, square face, thick black moustache, receding black hair combed back with pomade, small tired eyes. He wears an untucked short-sleeved batik shirt with a brown-and-navy floral coastal batik pattern, grey trousers, a black leather belt, a steel wristwatch and brown leather sandals. He has a gentle, slightly sheepish manner, often rubbing the back of his neck.
