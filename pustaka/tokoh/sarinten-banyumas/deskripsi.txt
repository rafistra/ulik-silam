Sarinten, a 30-year-old Javanese woman from Banyumas, a former ronggeng dancer now working as a gowok. Slender and of medium height, warm medium-brown skin, oval face, softly arched dark brows, calm dark-brown almond-shaped eyes, small straight nose, full lips. Long black hair combed back into a low round bun at the nape, held with a plain dark wooden hairpin. She wears a long-sleeved, hip-length kebaya of plain dark indigo cotton without embroidery, closed at the front with three small brass pins, over a high cream cotton under-bodice; an ankle-length wrapped kain of hand-drawn batik in Banyumas sogan colours, dark brown and black on an aged-cream ground, with a bold lumbon motif (large stylized taro leaves with broad veins); bare feet. A thin plain silver bangle on her left wrist and small gold stud earrings. She carries herself with a dancer's upright, unhurried grace.
