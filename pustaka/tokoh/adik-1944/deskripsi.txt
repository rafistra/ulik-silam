Adik: a Javanese boy of 13 from Surabaya, the younger brother of Romusha Surabaya, a forced laborer on Noemfoor Island in 1944. Small, very thin frame with ribs and elbows showing; warm medium-brown skin a shade lighter than his sister's; a round face with large dark eyes, short straight brows, a small nose and chapped lips. Short black hair roughly cropped close to the scalp, uneven. Bare-chested, with a faded white cotton shirt far too big for him knotted around his waist; knee-length dark grey cotton shorts, torn at one leg and held up with a twisted cloth cord. Barefoot, with coral scratches on his shins. No jewelry, no headwear. He stays close to his sister, often clutching the edge of her sleeve, his curious eyes darting around even when he is afraid.
