Character reference sheet of Kiai Utusan.
Kiai Utusan, a Javanese kiai of about 50 from rural East Java in October 1945, the delegate of his regional NU consul. Medium height, sturdy build with a slight belly, warm brown skin, broad calm face, deep-set dark eyes with smile lines, heavy greying brows, broad nose, a neatly trimmed grey-flecked chin beard and no moustache. A white cotton turban wound around a white skullcap; a long charcoal-grey collarless jacket buttoned to the neck over a white shirt; an ankle-length hand-woven cotton sarung in brown and black checks; simple brown leather sandals. A worn string of dark wooden prayer beads in his right hand, a folded paper letter tucked in his jacket. He moves slowly and deliberately, often resting one hand on his chest while he listens.
Full-body front, side and back views in a relaxed natural standing pose; one three-quarter face portrait; one detail panel of the turban, the collarless jacket buttons, the checked sarung and the prayer beads.
