Teknisi Radio: a Javanese young man of about 20 from Surabaya, the radio technician of the Republican fighters' secret broadcasting station in 1945. Lean, wiry build; warm medium-brown skin; an angular face with high cheekbones, dark deep-set eyes, thick straight brows, a broad nose and a faint shadow of a mustache. Short black hair, side-parted and combed back. He wears a white short-sleeved cotton shirt with the sleeves rolled up tight above the elbow, open at the collar, a small red-and-white cloth badge pinned above the left breast pocket, knee-length khaki cotton shorts with a plain canvas belt, and black canvas shoes without socks. A small screwdriver clipped in his breast pocket and a pair of 1940s bakelite headphones hanging around his neck. He moves fast and economically, always adjusting a dial or a wire, glancing often toward the door.
