Wage Rudolf Supratman, a 25-year-old Javanese journalist and violinist in Batavia, 1928. Slim, narrow-shouldered build of average height; light brown skin; an oval face with a soft jaw, high cheekbones and a gentle mouth; dark almond-shaped eyes behind round thin black wire-rimmed glasses; straight dark brows; clean-shaven. Thick wavy black hair combed back, its waves showing at the temples and nape under a black peci covered in fine dark-striped lurik woven cotton. A single-breasted dark brown cotton lounge jacket with narrow lapels over a white collared shirt and a plain dark tie, high-waisted dark grey trousers with a sharp crease, and polished brown leather lace-up shoes. No jewelry. Carries a worn dark brown wooden violin case with brass clasps. Holds himself slightly stooped, quiet and watchful, his long fingers often resting on the violin case.
