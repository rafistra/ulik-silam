Character reference sheet of Anak.
Anak, a Javanese-Dutch boy of about two to three. Small and round-cheeked, with light olive skin clearly lighter than his mother's, soft light-brown curly hair, large dark brown eyes and a small nose. He wears a loose knee-length white cotton smock with short sleeves and goes barefoot; in some scenes he also wears a small white cloth European-style cap. No jewelry. Clings to adults and reaches out with chubby hands; curious and often sleepy.
Full-body front, side and back views in a relaxed natural standing pose, one three-quarter portrait of the face, and one detail panel of the curly hair with and without the small white cap.
Plain light background. Detailed vector art illustration, clean crisp outlines, flat colors with subtle shading, historically accurate 1880s child's dress in a Dutch colonial household.
