Opas, a fictional Betawi native police assistant (oppas) in Batavia, 1892 to 1893, about 25 years old. Lean wiry build, medium-brown sun-darkened skin, oval face with high cheekbones, dark watchful almond eyes under straight thick brows, broad nose, thin lips, clean-shaven, short black hair. Clothing from head to foot: a dark indigo cotton iket head cloth tied flat with a small knot at the back; a plain dark indigo collarless cotton jacket with long sleeves, narrow buttoned cuffs and small brass buttons down the front; a worn brown leather belt over the jacket with a small cartridge pouch; loose dark indigo knee-length trousers; barefoot. Carries an old single-shot rifle with a plain wooden stock. No jewelry. Holds himself stiffly, shoulders slightly hunched, eyes often glancing sideways.
