Character reference sheet of Ronin. Ronin, a Japanese masterless samurai about thirty years old, hired as a VOC mercenary in 1621. Lean and wiry, medium height, sun-browned light skin, narrow face with sharp cheekbones, watchful dark eyes under straight heavy brows, thin lips, a few days of black stubble. Black hair in a rough topknot with the crown grown out unshaven and loose strands at the temples. A faded indigo cotton kosode with narrow sleeves and frayed cuffs, closed left over right, under a dark grey-brown hemp hakama tied at the waist with a plain black sash; shins wrapped in off-white cloth, straw waraji sandals. A katana and a shorter wakizashi in worn black lacquered scabbards thrust through the sash, plain round iron guards, dark cord-wrapped hilts. His hands are lean and sun-browned, knuckles scarred and palms callused from years of sword practice, short square nails with grime under them, a thin pale scar across the back of the right hand; forearms covered by the narrow sleeves of a faded indigo cotton kosode with frayed cuffs, no rings or jewelry. Carries himself quietly, weight often on his back foot, eyes always measuring the people around him.
