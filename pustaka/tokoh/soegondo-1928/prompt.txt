Soegondo Djojopoespito, a Javanese law student in his early twenties and chairman of the Second Youth Congress, Batavia, 1928. Medium height with a compact, upright build; light brown skin; a broad face with a square jaw, straight nose and full lips; calm dark eyes under thick straight brows; clean-shaven, no glasses.
