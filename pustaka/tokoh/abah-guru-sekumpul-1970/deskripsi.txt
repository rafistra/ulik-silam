Abah Guru Sekumpul (KH Muhammad Zaini bin Abdul Ghani), a Banjar Islamic scholar from Martapura, South Kalimantan; his age changes by scene (28 in 1970, 48 in 1990, 63 in 2005). Medium height with a slim, slender build and narrow shoulders, fair light-tan skin, a slightly oval, gently elongated face with smooth cheeks, completely clean-shaven with no beard and no moustache, gentle narrow dark eyes, straight dark brows, a small rounded nose and a soft closed-lip smile; short dark hair visible only at the temples. He has two looks. Young look (1970s, age about 28): no eyeglasses; a round white knitted kopiah (prayer cap) instead of a turban; a plain collarless long-sleeved white shirt; a smoother, youthful face. Mature look (from about 1990, ages 48 to 63): thin gold metal-rimmed aviator-style eyeglasses with a double bridge; a large rounded white turban wrapped smoothly in many layers, with no hanging tail; a long plain white jubah (ankle-length robe) with a high stand-up collar and long wide sleeves; a dark green shawl draped over his right shoulder and hanging straight down the front. Simple sandals in both looks. He carries a small kitab (Arabic prayer book) and a string of dark wooden prayer beads. Calm, unhurried bearing: shoulders relaxed, head slightly tilted when listening, hands resting quietly folded in front.
