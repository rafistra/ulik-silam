Lean, wiry build of medium height, deep sun-darkened brown skin, angular face with high cheekbones, dark brown narrow eyes with fine squint lines, straight black eyebrows, broad nose, a thin black moustache, short black hair cut close at the sides and flattened by his hat. A heavy single-edged golok cane knife in a plain wooden sheath tied at the left hip with a cord. Calloused hands with small pale cane-leaf scratches, no jewelry. Quiet, watchful eyes and slightly stooped shoulders from long hours bent over the cane.
