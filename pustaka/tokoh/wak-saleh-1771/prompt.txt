Character reference sheet of Wak Saleh.
Wak Saleh, a Malay river helmsman in his late fifties from the Mempawah coast of West Borneo, 1771. Thin, sinewy and slightly stooped; deep brown weathered skin; narrow face with creased hollow cheeks, hooded heavy-lidded eyes, sparse grey eyebrows, a thin grey moustache and a wisp of grey chin beard; lips stained red-brown from chewing betel; short grey hair. A plain dark brown cotton destar headcloth knotted at the side of the head. A faded dark brown collarless cotton shirt hanging open at the chest; a dark green and black checked cotton sarong hitched to the knee; barefoot. A small plain cloth pouch for betel tucked into the waist of the sarong; no jewelry. Sits low and still at the stern, both hands firm on the long steering oar, eyes always on the riverbank.
