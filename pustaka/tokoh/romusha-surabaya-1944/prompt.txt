Character reference sheet of Romusha Surabaya.
Romusha Surabaya: a Javanese woman of about 20 from Surabaya, a commoner forced laborer on Noemfoor Island in 1944. Slight, undernourished build with visible collarbones; warm medium-brown skin; oval face with high cheekbones, dark almond-shaped eyes, thick straight brows, a broad soft nose and full lips cracked from thirst. Long black hair twisted into a loose low bun at the nape, with strands falling free. She wears a plain hip-length off-white cotton kebaya blouse, now torn and stained grey-brown, its sleeves ripped ragged just below the elbow, held closed with two plain safety pins; around her hips, a faded brown cotton kain wrap with a small stamped motif of scattered dots and tiny flowers almost washed out, its hem frayed and torn to mid-calf; a thin strip of burlap sacking tied around her waist as a belt. Barefoot, her feet cut by coral. No jewelry. She moves with guarded, watchful alertness, shoulders slightly hunched, always half-turned toward the younger brother she protects.
