Radin, a Javanese santri (Islamic boarding-school student) in his twenties, from a poor farming family on the north coast of Central Java, mid-19th century. Slim, wiry build of a young man used to walking long distances; warm medium-brown skin; long oval face with prominent high cheekbones and a slightly tapering jaw, straight dark eyebrows, narrow dark attentive eyes, a broad nose, thin lips, noticeably large protruding ears, clean-shaven with no moustache or beard; short black hair hidden beneath the headcloth. Head: a plain undyed off-white cotton iket headcloth tied simply around the head with the ends tucked in. Body: a loose collarless long-sleeved baju shirt of faded indigo cotton, sleeves ending in a plain wide cuff. Lower body: a checked cotton sarong in muted brown and dark blue, folded and tucked at the waist, falling to the ankles. Barefoot. Carries a small cloth bundle over one shoulder. No jewelry, no weapon. Leans slightly forward with eager curiosity, often holding a small hand-copied book against his chest.
