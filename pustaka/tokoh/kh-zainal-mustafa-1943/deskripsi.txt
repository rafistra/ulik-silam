KH Zainal Mustafa, a Sundanese Muslim cleric about 44 years old, founder and teacher of Pesantren Sukamanah, Singaparna, Tasikmalaya, West Java, in 1943–1944. Slim build, very upright posture, warm light-brown skin; oval youthful face, calm dark eyes under straight thick brows, straight nose, full lips, clean-shaven with no moustache and no beard; black hair at the temples. Wears a white cloth turban (sorban) wound neatly around the head with its loose end hanging down at one side of the neck; a light ochre collarless long-sleeved cotton shirt with thin golden-yellow piping along the neckline and front opening; a dark brown checked cotton sarong; barefoot or in simple leather sandals. No jewelry. Stands and sits very straight and still, gestures with an open palm, gaze steady and unhurried.
