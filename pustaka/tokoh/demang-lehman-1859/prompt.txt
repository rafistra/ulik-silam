Character reference sheet of Demang Lehman. Kiai Demang Lehman (born Idis), a Banjar war leader of about 27, South Kalimantan, 1859, trusted aide and commander for Prince Hidayatullah; his face follows the only known photograph of him (1864), drawn about five years younger. Lean, wiry build of medium height with narrow shoulders, warm brown skin; a narrow face with prominent cheekbones and slightly hollow cheeks, large wide-set dark eyes under straight dark brows drawn slightly together, a broad flat nose, full lips held firmly closed, clean-shaven with no beard and no moustache; short black hair with a few loose strands falling to the neck below the headcloth. Head: a dark green cotton headcloth with a small faded pale print, wrapped around the head with one loose end hanging at the side. Body: a plain white collarless long-sleeved cotton shirt under a collarless dark green cotton jacket fastened down the front with a row of small round buttons; a thin plain cord around the neck. Waist: a wide undyed cotton sash holding a kris in a plain dark wooden sheath, over loose ankle-length cotton trousers with a small dotted print. Barefoot. Carries a long spear with a dark wooden shaft and a slim iron blade bound with a red cloth tassel. Calm, still and commanding, moves with economy and never hurries.
