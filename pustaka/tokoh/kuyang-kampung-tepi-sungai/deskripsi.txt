Kuyang, a flying-head spirit of Banjar folk belief in South Kalimantan, only ever seen at night: the detached head of a woman of uncertain age floating in the air with no body. Gaunt grey-brown skin stretched tight over sharp cheekbones; deep-set bloodshot red eyes with pinpoint pupils; thin cracked lips pulled back over two long pointed upper fangs; a long dark tongue; very long, soaking-wet, tangled black hair hanging down around and below the face. Beneath the jaw, where the neck should be, a dark tangled mass hangs down like wet roots, its shapes lost in shadow. A dull red ember-like glow clings around her. No clothing, no jewelry. Hovers perfectly still, head slightly tilted, as if listening.
