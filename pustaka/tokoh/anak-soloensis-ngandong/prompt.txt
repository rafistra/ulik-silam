Character reference sheet of Anak Soloensis.
Anak Soloensis is a Homo erectus child of about six from the same band. Small and thin, dark brown skin, a rounded low forehead with only faint brow ridges, large curious dark eyes, a wide flat nose and a small jaw without a chin; short, dark, tightly curled hair. A small wrap of soft brown hide around the hips; barefoot. A tiny chipped stone is often clutched in one fist. Curious and clinging, quick to copy the adults.
Full-body front, side and back views in a relaxed, natural standing pose, one three-quarter face portrait, and one detail panel of the small chipped stone in the child's hand. Plain light background.
