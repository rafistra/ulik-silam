Sahabat Montir, a Javanese young man about 23 years old, the mechanic's best friend, in Yogyakarta in 1983. Stocky, broad-shouldered build, warm dark-brown skin; round face, wide easy grin, slightly wavy black hair reaching the collar, a thin moustache. A faded blue-black tattoo of a coiled snake on the outer left upper arm. Wears a red-and-white horizontally striped cotton T-shirt with the sleeves cut off, faded blue jeans and black rubber flip-flops. Loose, cheerful posture.
