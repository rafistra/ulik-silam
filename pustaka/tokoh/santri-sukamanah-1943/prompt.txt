Character reference sheet of Santri Sukamanah.
Santri Sukamanah, a Sundanese pesantren student about 17 years old at Pesantren Sukamanah, Singaparna, Tasikmalaya, West Java, in 1943–1944. Slim, wiry build with warm light-brown skin; oval face, dark brown eyes, straight thick brows, short straight nose, full lips, a faint shadow of a first moustache; short black hair under a plain black velvet peci (a flat-topped oval cap). Wears a faded white collarless long-sleeved cotton shirt with three cloth-covered buttons at the neck, sleeves often rolled below the elbows; a dark green-and-brown checked cotton sarong tied at the waist; barefoot or in worn wooden clog sandals. A small black wooden prayer-bead string (tasbih) wrapped twice around his right wrist; no other jewelry. Quiet and attentive, weight often on the balls of his feet as if ready to move.
