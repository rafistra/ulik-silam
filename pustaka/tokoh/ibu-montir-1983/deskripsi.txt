Ibu Montir, a Javanese mother about 50 years old in a kampung in Yogyakarta in 1983. Small, thin build, warm brown skin; soft round face with lines around the eyes, gentle dark eyes, a small nose; black hair streaked with grey pulled back in a small low bun. Wears a plain pale green long-sleeved cotton kebaya blouse and a brown-and-indigo batik wrap skirt (jarik); barefoot or in simple sandals. A thin plain gold ring on the right hand. Moves quietly, hands often clasped.
