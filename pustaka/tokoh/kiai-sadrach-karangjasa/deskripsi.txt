Kiai Sadrach, a Javanese Christian community leader in his late thirties to forties, Karangjasa (Bagelen, Central Java), 1870s–1880s. Medium height, solid upright build; warm medium-brown skin; long oval face with prominent high cheekbones and a slightly tapering jaw, faint horizontal lines across the forehead, straight dark eyebrows that rise slightly toward the center, narrow dark eyes with a steady, stern gaze, a broad nose, thin firmly closed lips, noticeably large protruding ears, a clean-shaven upper lip with no moustache, and a narrow pointed tuft of beard on the chin only, black with a few grey streaks. Head: a batik iket headcloth in brick red and cream with a large organic pattern, wrapped in the Javanese blangkon manner with a pointed fold at the front and a small knot at the back. Body: a plain black long-sleeved Javanese jacket without stripes or pattern, high closed stand collar, closed at the front with small cloth buttons, sleeves ending in a narrow fitted cuff. Lower body: a wrapped batik jarik cloth in soga brown and indigo with a small repeating dotted-geometric pattern, falling to the ankles, tied with a plain dark sash. Barefoot or simple leather sandals. No cross pendant, no jewelry. Holds himself very upright and still, gestures with open palms, a teacher's patient gaze.
