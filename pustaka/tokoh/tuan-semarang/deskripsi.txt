Tuan, a Dutch trading-company clerk of about 36 in 1880s Semarang. Tall and lean with slightly stooped shoulders; pale skin reddened by the tropical sun; a long narrow face, light grey-blue eyes, pale brows, a straight thin nose and thin lips under a neatly trimmed sandy-brown moustache; short sandy-brown hair parted on the side. At home he wears a collarless white cotton shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbow and loose ankle-length trousers of thin batik cloth printed with red and blue flowers, with leather slippers; for official business, a white closed-collar cotton jacket buttoned to the throat, matching white trousers and black leather shoes. A silver watch chain; often a cigar in hand. He moves slowly and deliberately, chin slightly raised, rarely looking directly at the people who serve him.
