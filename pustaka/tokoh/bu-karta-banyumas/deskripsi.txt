Bu Karta, a 45-year-old Javanese merchant's wife from Banyumas in the late 1930s. Short and plump, light-brown skin, round face with full cheeks, thin neatly shaped brows, sharp attentive dark eyes, a few grey streaks in black hair worn in a neat low bun with a small gold hairpin. She wears a long-sleeved, hip-length kebaya of fine cream cotton with a narrow hand-made lace edge along the front, fastened with a set of three linked gold brooches; an ankle-length wrapped kain of sogan batik, dark brown and black on cream, with a sekar surya motif (open flowers with petals radiating like sun rays); a plain dark brown selendang over one shoulder; flat leather sandals. Two thin gold bangles on each wrist and gold drop earrings. She carries herself with a composed, dignified stiffness.
