Schout A.W.V. Hinne, Dutch colonial police chief of the Tanah Abang district in Batavia, early 1890s, in his late thirties (face invented, no portrait known). Tall and heavy-set, ruddy sunburned pale skin, square jaw, small pale grey eyes, thick drooping light-brown moustache, short receding light-brown hair. Clothing from head to foot: a white cotton cap with a short black visor; a white high-collared closed tunic with a single row of brass buttons; a black leather belt with a revolver holster on the right hip and a sabre on the left; white trousers; black leather ankle boots; a pocket-watch chain across the chest. Moves restlessly, leans forward when listening, jaw often clenched.
