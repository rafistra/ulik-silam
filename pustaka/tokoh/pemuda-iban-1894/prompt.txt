Character reference sheet of Pemuda Iban.
Pemuda Iban is an Iban man of about twenty from the Batang Lupar river region of western Borneo, in 1894, a paddler and porter on a Dutch expedition. Lean, wiry build, warm medium-brown skin, an oval face with high cheekbones, lively dark eyes, thick straight brows, a broad nose and full lips. His thick black hair is cut in a straight square fringe across the forehead and falls long and loose down his back past the shoulders; the sides are not shaved. A row of small brass rings runs along the rim of each ear. On each shoulder, just below the collarbone, sits a dark blue round tattoo shaped like an eggplant blossom with a double spiral at its centre. He is bare-chested and wears a long waistcloth of faded red cotton wound around the waist and passed between the legs, its front flap hanging to the knees; barefoot. A plain white shell bracelet circles his right wrist, and a working machete in a plain wooden sheath hangs at his left hip from a rattan belt. Quick and watchful, always half-turned toward the others.
