Ibu 1998, a 50-year-old Javanese farming mother from a village in Klaten, Central Java, 1998. Slim and of medium height, a straight back and wiry arms from field work; warm brown skin weathered by the sun; oval face with soft cheekbones and fine lines, calm dark eyes with gentle crow's feet, a broad nose, thin lips, a small mole on the left cheek below the eye; black hair parted in the middle, the front visible under a long beige cotton selendang scarf loosely draped over the head, one end thrown back over the left shoulder. Wears a long-sleeved hip-length kebaya blouse of cream cotton printed with faded pink rose flowers and grey-green leaves, a brown batik sarong with a small dotted floral pattern wrapped to the ankles, and plain black rubber flip-flops. Moves with quiet patience, often clutching the end of her selendang with one hand.
