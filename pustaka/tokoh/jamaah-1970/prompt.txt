Character reference sheet of Jamaah.
Jamaah, a fictional Banjar market trader from Martapura; shown at three ages (early twenties in 1970, forties in the 1980s and 1990, seventies from 2000 on). Medium-brown skin, a lean wiry build, a long face with deep-set dark eyes, thick brows, a broad nose and a thin moustache; short black hair that turns grey in the 1990s and white by 2005, under a black velvet peci (songkok). Wears a plain cream or white long-sleeved cotton shirt (a white koko shirt from the 1980s on), a checked sarong in dark green and brown, and rubber sandals; carries a woven rattan market basket in youth and a wooden walking stick in old age. A thin woven-rattan bracelet on the left wrist in every age, a plain silver ring on the right ring finger from the 1980s on. Patient, watchful, hands often resting on his knees.
