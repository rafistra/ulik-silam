Reference sheet of Prasasti Kawali I.
Prasasti Kawali I, an Old Sundanese stone inscription from Kawali, West Java, 14th century. A broad, flat, roughly trapezoidal natural andesite slab, slightly taller than wide, reaching about the hip of an adult, only a hand-span thick; dark grey with green-grey lichen and moss patina; undressed, irregular natural edges; about ten uneven horizontal lines of shallow incised script on the broad face and a few short lines along the thick side edge, shown as faint illegible marks; set upright in the earth; no frame, no border, no polish.
Front view, side view showing the thin edge, and three-quarter view of the whole stone, plus one close-up panel of the weathered surface with faint illegible incised marks. One small human figure standing beside the stone for scale. Plain light background.
