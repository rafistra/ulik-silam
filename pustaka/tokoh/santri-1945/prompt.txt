Character reference sheet of Santri.
Santri, an 18-year-old Javanese pesantren student from rural East Java in October 1945, the quiet assistant of his kiai. Slim, wiry build, warm medium-brown skin, oval face, dark almond-shaped eyes, thick straight brows, small flat-bridged nose, full lips, a faint first moustache; short black hair mostly hidden under his cap. A plain black velvet peci, elliptical and slightly worn at the rim; a plain off-white long-sleeved collarless cotton shirt with a short front placket of three cloth-covered buttons, sleeves rolled once at the wrists; an ankle-length hand-woven cotton sarung in faded indigo and brown checks, folded and rolled at the waist; bare feet. A coarse undyed cotton shoulder bag (his kiai's bag) slung across his chest, a small string of wooden prayer beads tucked into the waist roll of the sarung. He carries himself slightly hunched and attentive, with restless hands that keep checking the bag strap.
