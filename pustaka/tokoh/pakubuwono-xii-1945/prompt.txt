Character reference sheet of Pakubuwono XII.
Pakubuwono XII, Susuhunan of Surakarta, about 20 years old in 1945, Javanese. Slim, upright build, light brown skin, smooth oval face, calm dark almond eyes, straight brows, short neat black hair under his headgear, clean-shaven, youthful but reserved look. Coronation dress: a tall black velvet kuluk: a brimless cap shaped like a truncated cone narrowing to a flat top, with gold braided trim along the bottom edge, a horizontal gold braid band about two-thirds up, vertical gold braid strips dividing the upper part into panels, and a small beaded gold knob on top; a dark close-fitting high-collared court jacket with dense gold embroidery along the edges and cuffs; a very large wrapped ceremonial waist-cloth with bold diagonal blade-shaped stripes (parang barong) around a plain diamond-shaped centre panel, its long pointed tail trailing behind; patterned silk trousers (cindhe) visible below; slippers. A keris worn at the back of the waist, small ornamental gold ear pieces. Sits and stands very still and composed, hands resting on his knees, speaking little.
