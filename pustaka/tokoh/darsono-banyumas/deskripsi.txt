Darsono, a 20-year-old Javanese man, son of a well-off Banyumas merchant family in the late 1930s. Slim and slightly lanky, light-brown skin of someone who rarely works in the sun, youthful oval face, clean-shaven, thick straight black brows, wide shy dark eyes, short black hair under a headcloth. He wears an iket headcloth of dark brown batik with a small cream dotted pattern, folded and tied flat around the head with the knot at the back; a long-sleeved collarless jacket of hand-woven lurik cotton with thin vertical stripes in muted brown, charcoal and cream, buttoned down the front; an ankle-length wrapped kain of sogan batik, brown and black on cream, with a pring sedapur motif (clusters of upright bamboo stems and leaves); simple dark leather slip-on sandals. No jewelry except a plain silver ring on his right hand. He holds his shoulders slightly hunched and his hands restless.
