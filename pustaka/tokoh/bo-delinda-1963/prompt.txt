Character reference sheet of Bo' Delinda'.
Bo' Delinda', a Wehea Dayak elder leader from Muara Wahau, East Kalimantan, in 1963, a man of about sixty. Stocky, broad-shouldered build; deep brown weathered skin; round, broad face with heavy cheeks; heavy-lidded calm dark eyes; sparse grey brows; wide flat nose; full lips set in a straight line; deep lines from nose to mouth; close-cropped grey hair; earlobes unstretched. He wears a collarless long-sleeved cotton shirt dyed dark indigo, a plain dark brown cotton sarong wrapped at the waist and falling to mid-calf, a narrow plain dark cloth headband tied at the back, and goes barefoot. A small woven rattan shoulder bag hangs at his right side. He sits very still with both hands resting on his knees, speaks slowly, and looks steadily at the person in front of him.
