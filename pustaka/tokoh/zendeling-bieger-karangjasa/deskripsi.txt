Zendeling Bieger, a Dutch Reformed missionary in his thirties to forties, Purworejo (Central Java), 1880s. Tall, thin, slightly stooped; pale skin reddened by the sun; long narrow face, deep-set light grey-blue eyes, straight thin nose, thin lips; a full brown beard trimmed close at the sides, short brown hair parted at the side. Wears a black wool frock coat reaching the knees, a white shirt with a stiff high collar and a narrow black necktie, a dark waistcoat, dark trousers and black leather shoes. Carries a black-bound Bible. Holds himself stiffly with his chin raised, hands often clasped behind his back.
