Sultan Malik az-Zahir, the Muslim ruler of Samudera Pasai on the north coast of Sumatra, a Malay jurist-king of about 45 in 1345. Sturdy medium build, warm medium-brown skin, a broad calm face with a high forehead, dark brown almond-shaped eyes, straight thick eyebrows, a short neat black beard and moustache, lips faintly reddened by betel. His Friday jurist dress: a plain white cotton turban, an ankle-length plain white cotton robe with long sleeves, an off-white cotton cloth wrapped around the hips beneath it instead of trousers, and simple leather sandals. His royal dress: a deep crimson silk tunic with a narrow gold border over a cream cotton under-tunic, a dark green silk hip-wrap with a gold border, and a white silk turban with a thin gold band. One plain gold ring on the right hand, no weapon. Calm and attentive, he leans slightly forward when he listens.
