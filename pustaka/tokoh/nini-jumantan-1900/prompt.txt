Character reference sheet of Nini Jumantan.
Nini Jumantan, a Banjar village midwife (bidan kampung) in her early sixties from a riverside kampung in South Kalimantan, around 1900. Small, wiry and slightly stooped but steady; deep brown weathered skin; a narrow lined face with high cheekbones, calm heavy-lidded dark eyes, thin grey eyebrows and a firm small mouth, lips stained red-brown from chewing betel; grey-streaked black hair pulled up into a high rounded bun with the ends tucked away (gelung pucung), no head covering indoors. A plain long-sleeved kebaya (kubaya) of dark brown cotton without lace, closed at the front; a batik hip-wrap (tapih bakurung) in muted brown and ochre with small scattered flower motifs, wrapped tightly at the waist and covered by the kebaya hem; barefoot. Small gold stud earrings set with dark glass stones; no other jewelry. Moves slowly and surely, hands always busy, eyes always reading the room.
Full-body front, side and back views in a relaxed natural standing pose, one three-quarter face portrait with a calm, knowing expression, and one detail panel of the high rounded hair bun, the kebaya front, the small flower motif of the batik hip-wrap and the earrings. Plain light background.
Detailed vector art illustration, clean crisp outlines, flat colors with subtle shading, faithful to everyday dress of older Banjar village women in South Kalimantan around 1900: a plain dark kebaya, a brown and ochre batik hip-wrap and a high rounded bun.
