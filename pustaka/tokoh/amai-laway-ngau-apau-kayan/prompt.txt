Lean, sinewy build of an upriver farmer and boatman; warm medium-brown skin; long face with high cheekbones; narrow deep-set dark eyes; straight thick brows; broad flat nose; thin firm lips; faint lines at the eye corners; straight black hair cut short with a few grey strands; no tattoos; earlobes slightly stretched with no earrings.
