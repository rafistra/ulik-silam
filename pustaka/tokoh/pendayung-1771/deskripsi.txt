Pendayung, a young Malay river paddler of about twenty from the Mempawah coast of West Borneo, a follower in a founder's boat party in 1771. Lean, wiry build; warm brown sun-darkened skin; oval face with wary dark eyes under straight thick brows, a broad nose and full lips; short black hair mostly hidden. A faded indigo plain cotton destar headcloth tied simply around the head with a small knot at the back. A loose collarless undyed off-white cotton shirt with sleeves rolled to just below the elbow; a checked cotton sarong in dark red and brown squares, hitched up and tucked at the waist so it falls to the knees; barefoot. A thin plaited rattan cord bracelet on the right wrist; no other jewelry and no weapon. Carries himself watchful and tense, shoulders slightly hunched, always gripping something.
