Anak Petani, a Javanese village girl about 9 years old from Kemusu, Boyolali, Central Java, in the late 1980s. Small and thin, warm brown skin; round face, large dark brown eyes, short straight brows, small rounded nose, slightly chapped lips; straight black shoulder-length hair held back by a plain red plastic hair clip on the left side. Wears a faded pink short-sleeved cotton blouse with a small round collar and two white buttons, and a knee-length plain navy-blue cotton skirt; barefoot or in thin blue rubber sandals. No jewelry. Moves quickly and curiously, often clutching something to her chest, and tilts her head when she asks a question.
