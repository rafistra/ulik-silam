Character reference sheet of Kuntilanak.
Kuntilanak, a female spirit of Malay folklore in West Borneo, 1771, only ever glimpsed and never clearly seen. A tall, very thin woman's figure; pale grey-white skin; her face always completely hidden behind very long straight black hair that falls past her knees. A long, loose, plain off-white cotton garment, simple and old, its ragged hem drifting as if underwater. Long thin pale arms hanging at her sides; bare pale feet that never quite touch the ground; no jewelry. Utterly still, head slightly tilted, as if listening.
